About servicing

Regular servicing keeps your car safe, reliable and worth more when you sell it. Most problems we see could have been caught early. A worn brake pad or a weeping hose is cheap to fix at a service and expensive on the side of the road.

We service to your manufacturer's logbook schedule using genuine or equivalent-quality parts and fluids. Under Australian Consumer Law, that keeps your new-car warranty intact. You don't have to go back to the dealer.

Before we start, we confirm the price with you. If we find anything else that needs doing, we call you with photos and a quote, and we don't do the work until you say yes.

What we do and how

  • Engine oil and filterDrained warm so the old oil carries contaminants out, then refilled with the grade and spec your manufacturer lists. New filter and sump plug washer every time.
  • Air and cabin filtersInspected and replaced when due or when they're restricting airflow.
  • BrakesWheels come off. We measure pad and rotor thickness, clean and lubricate the calliper slides, and test brake fluid for moisture.
  • TyresTread depth measured on every tyre, pressures set to the placard, and tyres rotated when your schedule calls for it.
  • Steering and suspensionChecked on the hoist for play in ball joints, tie-rod ends and bushes, and for leaking shocks.
  • Cooling systemCoolant level and strength tested, hoses and radiator checked, and a pressure test if anything looks wrong.
  • Battery and chargingLoad tested, terminals cleaned, and alternator output checked.
  • Drive belts and hosesChecked for cracks, glazing and tension, and replaced before they fail.
  • Other fluidsTransmission, differential, power steering and washer fluids checked and topped up or changed as scheduled.
  • Lights, wipers and hornEvery bulb, both wipers and the horn tested. Blown bulbs are replaced on the spot.
  • Fault scan and service resetWe scan for stored fault codes, reset the service light and stamp your logbook.
  • Road testEvery car is driven after its service to check brakes, steering, noises and warning lights.

Service levels

Basic service

Oil and filter, fluid top-ups, tyre pressures, and a safety inspection of brakes, lights and suspension. Good between major services.

Logbook service

Everything your manufacturer lists for your car's age and kilometres, done in order and stamped in your logbook.

Major service

A logbook service plus spark plugs, filters, brake fluid and coolant where due, and a deeper inspection of the whole car.

Your service reminders

When you book a service online, your account works out when your next one is due: six months after your appointment. It then repeats every six months after that, assuming you keep coming back.

Your schedule carries on as long as you come back within two months of each due date. If two months pass without a visit, the reminders stop. Book your next service whenever you're ready and they start again from that date.

To log in, all you need is your first name and phone number.
