# Kingsway Auto Works price guide

Prices confirmed by workshop: no

All prices are in Australian dollars and include GST.
Labour rate: $140 per hour.
Times are working days (Monday to Friday) from drop-off, unless shown in hours.

## Servicing
- Basic service, small or medium car: $199 to $249. Same day, 2 to 3 hours.
- Basic service, large car, 4WD or van: $249 to $319. Same day, 2 to 3 hours.
- Logbook service, small or medium car: $249 to $399. Same day, 2 to 4 hours. Depends on which scheduled items are due.
- Logbook service, large car, 4WD or van: $329 to $549. Same day, 3 to 5 hours.
- Major service (spark plugs, brake fluid, coolant as due): $449 to $799. 1 day.
- Brake pads, one axle: $220 to $320. Same day, 1 to 2 hours.
- Brake pads and rotors, one axle: $450 to $750. Same day, 2 to 3 hours. Parts vary by car.
- Wheel alignment: $89 to $129. 1 hour.
- Tyre fitting and balancing, per tyre (tyre not included): $30 to $45.
- Air-conditioning re-gas and leak test: $150 to $250. Same day, 1 to 2 hours.
- Pre-purchase inspection: $199 to $259. Same day, 1 to 2 hours.
- Clutch replacement: from $1,200. 1 to 2 days. Varies a lot by car.

## Diagnosis
- Diagnostic scan and fault finding, first hour: $140 to $160. Further time at the labour rate, only with the customer's OK.

## Smash repair
Smash repair always needs photos or an inspection before a firm quote. Insurance jobs can take extra days while the insurer approves the repair.
- Paintless dent repair, per dent (paint not damaged): $120 to $350. Same day to 1 day.
- Scratch or scuff repair, per panel: $350 to $700. 2 to 3 days.
- Dent repair and repaint, per panel: $600 to $1,200. 3 to 5 days.
- Bumper repair and repaint: $500 to $900. 2 to 4 days.
- Bumper replacement and paint: $900 to $1,800. 3 to 7 days, depending on parts arriving.
- Headlight or tail light replacement: $150 to $300 labour, plus the light. Same day once the part arrives (1 to 5 days).
- Major collision (several panels, airbags or chassis): from $3,000. 2 to 6 weeks. Inspection required.

## Performance
- Dyno baseline, three power runs with printout: $150 to $220. 1 to 2 hours.
- Stage 1 ECU tune on the dyno: $800 to $1,500. 1 day.
- Cat-back exhaust, fitting only (parts extra): $250 to $450. Same day, 2 to 4 hours.
- Coilovers fitted with wheel alignment (parts extra): $450 to $750. 1 day.
- Front-mount intercooler fitted (parts extra): $500 to $900. 1 to 2 days.
- Turbo upgrade builds: from $6,000 including parts. 1 to 3 weeks. Consultation required.

## Things the assistant should not estimate
- Engine or gearbox rebuilds, electrical faults that need tracing, and anything not listed above. Offer to send a quote request instead.
